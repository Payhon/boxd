# Community code of conduct

boxd is committed to a respectful, constructive, and technically rigorous
community.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful across differences in experience, background, and opinion;
- critique ideas and evidence rather than people;
- make room for questions and good-faith mistakes;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, and sharing
  another person's private information;
- follow maintainer instructions when handling security-sensitive material.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the community. To report conduct concerns, contact the repository
owner privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled
with discretion and without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
